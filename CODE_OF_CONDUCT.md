# Code of Conduct

Tokenomics is a small hobby project. Everyone is welcome here, whatever their experience, background or language.

## Expected behaviour

- Be kind and patient. Many people here write in their second or third language.
- Assume good intent, and ask before you judge.
- Criticise code and ideas, never people.
- Accept that the maintainer decides what goes into the project, and why.

## Not acceptable

- Insults, harassment, threats or discriminatory jokes
- Personal attacks or deliberately hostile comments
- Publishing someone's private information without their permission
- Spam, advertising, or repeated off-topic posts

## Scope

This applies to everything in this repository: issues, pull requests, discussions, the project board and code comments.

## Reporting

If someone behaves in a way that breaks these rules, contact the maintainer **@Rheinwolf** on GitHub, or use GitHub's **Report content** option in the `…` menu of the comment or issue. Reports are handled confidentially.

## Consequences

Depending on how serious it is, the maintainer may remind someone of these rules, hide or delete comments, lock a conversation, or block a person from the repository.

This code of conduct is inspired by the [Contributor Covenant](https://www.contributor-covenant.org/).
